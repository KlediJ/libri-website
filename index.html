Can I still download Libri for free?

Yes. The current Windows version is available as a free download from GitHub. You can also find Libri Desktop in the Microsoft Store.

Is Libri 2.0 available now?

Not yet. Libri 2.0 is the upcoming paid version. The download links on this page are for the current version.

What will Libri 2.0 cost, and when will it launch?

Pricing and a release date haven’t been announced. We’ll share those details, along with the feature list, closer to launch.

Where does the current app keep my financial data?

The current Libri app stores your financial data locally on your computer. You import your transactions and manage your accounts, categories, and payees on your own device.
